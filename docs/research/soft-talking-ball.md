# Soft talking ball for turn-taking: idea note

Status: idea only. Nothing built or bought.

## Idea
A soft, throwable ball used in a talk or workshop. Whoever holds it speaks, and passing it passes the turn. Any recording or note-taking should only happen with the informed agreement of everyone taking part, announced beforehand, with a clear off switch and a plan for who can see or keep the notes; silent ambient recording is not part of the idea.

## Two goals, kept separate
1. Turn-taking: a plain soft ball already does this, with no electronics.
2. Listening and notes: the ball only signals who has the floor; any recording is optional, done by a phone or laptop only with participants' informed agreement and privacy safeguards.

## Options to compare
- Plain ball, no electronics (baseline).
- Ball with a small microcontroller and motion sensor that reports each pass to a phone.
- Phone-only version: tap the screen to pass the turn.

## Open questions
- Does the electronics add anything over the baseline?
- Parts cost and safety of a throwable device. Needs a dated price check before any figures are given.
